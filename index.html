About Me

Hands-on learner, builder & teacher

I'm Shakib from Bangladesh. My focus is Plumbing & Pipe Fitting — NSDA Level-02, Making Pipe Joints. I like to understand a tool by holding it, a joint by making it, and a system by testing it. That's why I turn what I learn into videos, exams and a full learning platform for other students.

🎯

My Goal

To become skilled, confident and job-ready in plumbing — and help other students learn the same practical way.

🛠️

How I Learn

Practice first. Tools, fittings, safety gear, measurements — then theory, exam practice and real work.

📚

What I Build

Learning platforms, online exams with picture questions, and Bengali video lessons for plumbing students.

Skills

What I can do

Practical skills I use every day — in the workshop and in digital learning.

🔧 Pipe Joints

Making strong, leak-free joints with elbows, tees, sockets and unions.

💧 Valves

Stopcock, ball, gate, butterfly, globe, check, foot, float and safety valves.

🛡️ Safety — OSH

Helmet, gloves, goggles, mask, apron, safety shoes and safe work practice.

🧰 Tools & Materials

Pipe cutter, wrench, Teflon tape, measuring and proper tool storage.

🎥 Learning Videos

Bengali video lessons with clear voice, subtitles and picture quizzes.

💻 Digital Learning

Online exams, picture MCQs, results and student dashboards.
